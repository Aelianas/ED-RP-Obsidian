Located in HIP 58832, Rackham's Peak is a small station on the far, upper edge of the galaxy. It's a beautiful place that requires a fleet carrier to reach. From the peak, you can look down at the entire Milky Way galaxy.

It boasts just 150,000 residents.

Almost monthly, Rackham's Peak holds a public holiday, in which they buy MILLIONS OF TONS of wine at nearly 300% the market value. This is when the [[Pilots Trade Network]] holds their monthly "Booze Cruise" event, by buying a bunch of cheap wine in the bubble, then carrying it all to the peak to allow pilots to deliver and sell it at huge profits.
