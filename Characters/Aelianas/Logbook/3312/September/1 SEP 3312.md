## #Date: 1 SEP 3312
## #Time: 00:00

#### #Entry:
As with [[31 AUG 3312]]'s log, this will be an ongoing log throughout the day. If there are significant gaps of more than two hours between entries, I will log the time before each section.

I have successfully met the quota I set to keep the [[Purple Cat Paradise (Fleet Carrier) (VFN-N5G)]] funded for the next year. The amount needed was just short of 1.88 billion credits. The fleet carrier's reserves are now at 1.95 billion credits and rising with each delivery.

I am current messaging a friend back in the bubble who is interested in becoming a pilot. She doesn't have the necessary credits or equipment to begin her career as a pilot yet, but the more I talk with her, the more interested she is in it. When I send her pictures I've taken of the outer reaches of the galaxy, her eyes light up brighter than all the collective stars that I'm looking down on. She's excited, to say the very least! I told her that when she has the equipment, I'll pay the cost of her pilot's license fee so that she can live her dreams of being a pilot.

#### #Time: 2:00:08

I have successfully gotten that friend to sign up for training for her pilot's license! She's currently going through trainings in the simulated systems we used to use prior to 3926. Once she has her bearings and can afford the equipment, she's going to actually take her pilot's license exam and join us in the black!

#### #Time: 16:13

I forgot to update my log last night before bed. After nearly 11.5 hours of hauling, I decided to take a break. I had a late night, talking with locals at [[Rackham's Peak]]. Everyone was having so much fun. I didn't drink anything, but everyone was so lively.

I'm exhausted today from having a late night, so I don't know how much hauling I'll be able to do today. I might take it easy for a little while today, until this brain fog clears.

Commander [[Aelianas]], signing off!
